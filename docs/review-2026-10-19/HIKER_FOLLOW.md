# Hiker following and overlapping fine terrain acquisition

The user clarified that diorama playback must follow the hiker, including MR. Previously following was desktop-only, opt-in and explicitly disabled during XR. Play now starts following in diorama mode. Desktop uses the existing raised inspection camera and sampled ridge clearance. XR translates the miniature to keep the moving hiker at a fixed room-space focus, 80 cm ahead and 45 cm below the initial viewing position. It never changes the tracked camera, model rotation or inspection scale. Head movement does not move the focus. Translation uses time-based smoothing with a 0.6 m/s cap so a scrub cannot teleport the model.

The spatial HUD replaces the diorama Center Hiker action with **Follow hiker / Stop following**. Pan, rotate, zoom, terrain dragging or one/two-hand model manipulation stop following. Pause retains the focus for inspection; Play resumes following. Route replacement, Fit/reset, view changes, reference-space reset and XR exit release ownership. Existing first-person behavior remains independent. During following, fine terrain prioritizes the hiker's current ground instead of an unrelated gaze intersection, while keeping broad visible anchors.

Fine terrain acquisition previously shared a serial lane with geometry generation and imagery promotion: even the next network request waited for publication preparation. A bounded queue now overlaps three detailed DEM requests on High, one on Balanced. Completed staging grids count toward that bound, so a stalled promotion cannot accumulate unbounded results. Geometry and atomic terrain/imagery publication remain serial, at most one section published per frame. Obsolete queued acquisitions are aborted; route disposal and profile replacement cancel the queue. Failure cooldowns, resident budgets, parent coverage, lossless source bytes and approximately 5 m High mesh cells remain intact.
