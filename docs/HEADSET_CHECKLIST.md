# Headset checklist (about 10 minutes, wearing the Quest 3)

These are the items from the review that need a person in the headset: hands, a real
table, or head movement. Everything else was checked on the device by driving it over
USB (see the progress log in `REVIEW_FIX_PLAN_2026_11_12.md`).

Open https://trekviewer.surge.sh/ in Quest Browser, pick any route, and use **Enter MR**
unless a step says otherwise. Note anything that looks wrong next to its item.

## Tabletop (MR, standing or sitting at a real table)

- [ ] **B4 Table placement.** With a Space Setup table, the model's base rests on the
  table top: not sunk into it, not floating.
- [ ] **X5 Two-hand zoom.** Grab the model with both hands and spread them. The base
  stays on the table, and the model stops growing at about 2.5 m wide.
- [ ] **X9 Release sound.** Let go of the model: a soft release sound plays.
- [ ] **X3 Near-field strip.** The small strip at the near edge of the model: poke
  Play/Pause, previous/next landmark and the scrub bar with your index finger. Each poke
  registers once.
- [ ] **B10 Poke release.** Press a panel button slowly and hold your fingertip on it:
  it fires once, and doesn't flicker or fire again while you hold.
- [ ] **X9 Panel resize.** Drag the panel by its top bar with your hand to a new
  distance and let go: it resizes to stay readable.
- [ ] **X2 Panel facing.** The docked panel is tilted to face your eyes (not just turned
  towards you), and no label spills out of its button.

## First person (life-size)

- [ ] **X1 Step movement.** With no saved settings, the left stick moves in 25 m steps,
  each with a brief dip to black (about 0.15 s) and no visible jump.
- [ ] **X6 Vignette.** With the comfort vignette on, turn smoothly. The dark ring looks
  the same in both eyes, with no doubled edge.
- [ ] **X7 Pause on Meta button.** Start the tour, press the Meta button: playback stops
  and the sticks do nothing until you come back.

## Guest test (optional, for sharing)

- [ ] Let someone who rarely uses VR watch the 90 s tour seated. Ask whether anything
  made them uncomfortable, and which controls confused them.
